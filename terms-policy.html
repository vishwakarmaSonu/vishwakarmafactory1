Terms & Conditions and Policy

These terms explain how this website, its product information, and its inquiry and feedback forms are used.

Terms & Conditions

Product descriptions, photographs, prices, finishes, and availability are provided for general information and may change. Natural wood grain, colour, and finish can vary between pieces. Please confirm the final product specifications, price, delivery arrangements, and expected timeline with Vishwakarma Arts before placing an order.

Submitting a booking or contact form is a request for information, not confirmation that an order has been accepted. An order is confirmed only after the product details, final price, payment arrangements, and delivery terms have been agreed with you.

For custom furniture, review and approve the design, measurements, materials, and finish before confirming the order. Any applicable change, cancellation, return, or refund arrangements will be communicated for the specific order and are subject to applicable law.

Please provide accurate contact and delivery information when using our forms. Do not copy, misuse, or interfere with website content or services.

Policy

When you contact us or submit a booking request, we may receive the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and product preferences. Feedback submissions may include your name, rating, and review.

We use these details to respond to inquiries, discuss and manage booking requests, and operate the feedback feature. Feedback, including the submitted name, rating, and review, may be displayed publicly in the customer reviews section. Please do not include private or sensitive information in a review.

Feedback submitted through this website is processed using the connected Google Apps Script and Google Sheet. Information submitted through other forms may be handled by the service providers used to operate those forms.

We retain submitted information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, manage the related request, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. To request access, correction, or removal of information, contact support@vishwakarmaart.in. Some records may need to be retained where required by law.
