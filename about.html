About Vishwakarma Art

Vishwakarma Art is a trusted name in handcrafted wooden furniture and home decor, bringing together traditional craftsmanship, modern design, and practical functionality. From elegant wooden temples and statement dining tables to thoughtful storage solutions and custom-made pieces, we create furniture that fits the rhythm of real homes and lifestyles.

We believe every piece should reflect quality, comfort, and personality. Our team works closely with customers to transform ideas into beautifully finished furniture that feels both timeless and personal.

About Us

Built on heritage and honesty

Based in Jodhpur, Vishwakarma Art is a family-driven furniture brand dedicated to creating durable, distinctive, and artistically crafted pieces for homes, offices, and spiritual spaces.
