About Vishwakarma Arts

Furniture is part of the stories we live in.

Vishwakarma Arts is a furniture maker and supplier based in Jodhpur, Rajasthan. Our team brings traditional woodworking sensibilities together with practical designs for modern homes.

From chairs and dining tables to beds, mirrors, and more, we make pieces with a focus on lasting utility and the natural character of wood.
